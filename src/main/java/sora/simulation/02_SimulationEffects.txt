Sora Dungeon Effects
========================

Purpose
- Effects are temporary or environmental modifiers applied to the player.
- Items, hazards, lights, enemies, and future scripted events can apply effects by effect_id.
- The runtime effect engine should keep active effects in a player-owned list.
- Each update, active effects are evaluated and their results are applied to character properties.
- The code-side effect package lives in sora.simulation.effects.
- DungeonCharacterState owns base properties, current properties, and the active-effect list.
- DungeonEffectLibrary owns known effect ids and public display names. Comfortable, Psychosis, Running, Eating, Restoration, Regeneration, Endurance Boost, Elevated Stamina, Starving, Food Poisoning, Toxic Exposure, and Asphyxiation have initial values; most other effects are placeholders until their behavior is decided.
- Effects with randomized or intentionally unknown duration should show ? in player-facing effect lists instead of the exact timer.
- Negative neutralizable effects can be removed by neutralization. Current examples include Food Poisoning, Poisoned, Toxic Exposure, Asphyxiation, Bleeding, Burning, Blinded, and Blast Trauma.

Active Effect Data
- effect_id: stable id for the effect definition.
- display_name: public-facing name shown to the player.
- duration: how long the effect remains active, in seconds.
- strength: numeric scale for the effect.
- mode: how the effect changes a property.

Effect Modes
- add: adds strength to the target property or rate while active.
- set: sets the target property to a specific value while active.

Character Properties Effects Can Touch
- health: player life.
- max_health: health cap.
- stamina: sprint/action fuel.
- max_stamina: stamina cap.
- stamina_regen: stamina recovered per second.
- movement_speed: movement multiplier.
- hunger: food pressure value.
- hunger_rate: hunger change per second.
- sanity: mental stability value.
- sanity_rate: sanity change per second.
- vision_radius: multiplier for the player visibility radius. Standard value is 1.0.
- noise: current sound output from 0 to 1000. Movement sets a fixed value while moving; interactions create short pulses. It does not accumulate as a meter.
- interaction_speed: multiplier for searching, opening, prying, and similar actions.

Baseline Character Rules
- Health starts at 100 and is capped by max_health.
- Stamina starts at 100, is capped by max_stamina, and currently regenerates at 6 per second.
- Running applies the running effect and drains stamina at 6 per second net.
- If stamina reaches 0 while running, running is blocked for 5 seconds.
- Hunger starts at 50 and drains at 2.5 per minute.
- If hunger is below 5, the player takes 5 health damage every 5 seconds.
- Hunger below 5 applies the persistent starving effect; starving owns the health damage.
- Food applies Eating, which restores hunger in 5 point chunks per second until that food's hunger_amount is spent.
- Food cannot be consumed while hunger is already full.
- When health is below max health, Restoration can start after 10 seconds without damage.
- Restoration heals 1 health per second at 100 hunger and scales proportionally down to 25 hunger.
- Restoration is disabled below 25 hunger, when health is full, or immediately after taking damage.
- Sanity starts at 100.
- Darkness drains sanity at 2.5 per minute.
- Lit areas restore sanity at 10 per minute.
- Sanity directly scales vision radius: 100 sanity is full radius, 0 sanity is 40 percent radius.
- If health reaches 0, the game stops updating and shows the death screen.
- Lit areas do not need a separate light exposure property.
- Lights should apply effects directly through their effect_id.
- Steady lights can apply lit.
- Flickering broken lights should not restore sanity.

Initial Effect Definitions
- These ids exist in the code-side DungeonEffectLibrary.
- Lit, darkness, running, eating, restoration, regeneration, elevated stamina, endurance boost, starving, sick, toxic exposure, and asphyxiation have initial behavior. Other effect modifiers are intentionally undecided until the effect behavior discussion is complete.

- lit
  - Display name: Comfortable.
  - Purpose: restore or stabilize sanity while the player is in a valid steady light radius.
  - Target: sanity_rate.
  - Mode: add.
  - Initial value: +10 sanity per minute.

- darkness
  - Display name: Psychosis.
  - Purpose: drain sanity while the player is not in a valid steady light radius.
  - Target: sanity_rate.
  - Mode: add.
  - Initial value: -2.5 sanity per minute.

- running
  - Display name: Running.
  - Purpose: drain stamina while the player is actively running.
  - Target: stamina_regen.
  - Mode: add.
  - Initial value: -12 stamina per second, which becomes -6 per second after the base +6 stamina regen.

- eating
  - Display name: Eating.
  - Purpose: restore hunger after consuming food.
  - Target: hunger.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: +5 hunger per second, capped by the consumed food's hunger_amount.

- restoration
  - Display name: Restoration.
  - Purpose: natural health recovery when the player is fed and has avoided damage.
  - Target: health.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: +1 health per second at 100 hunger, scaling by hunger percent down to 25 hunger.
  - Conditions: health below max, hunger at least 25, and no damage taken for 10 seconds.

- regeneration
  - Display name: Regeneration.
  - Purpose: unnatural healing from bandages or future healing items.
  - Target: health.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: +10 health every 5 seconds. Bandages should apply this for 15 seconds.

- elevated_stamina
  - Display name: Elevated Stamina.
  - Purpose: temporary stamina cap increase from endurance draught.
  - Target: max_stamina.
  - Mode: add.
  - Initial value: +50 max stamina, allowing stamina to cap at 150 while active.
  - Display timer: ? when duration is randomized.

- endurance_boost
  - Display name: Endurance Boost.
  - Purpose: temporary stamina recovery support from endurance draught.
  - Target: stamina_regen.
  - Mode: add.
  - Initial value: +5 stamina regen per second.
  - Display timer: ? when duration is randomized.

- elevated_health
  - Display name: Elevated Health.
  - Purpose: reserved for future temporary max-health increases.
  - Current behavior: placeholder.

- neutralization
  - Display name: Neutralization.
  - Purpose: antitoxin effect hook for removing neutralizable negative effects and restoring sanity.
  - Current behavior: item-use code should call neutralization behavior directly in the character state.

- starving
  - Display name: Starving.
  - Purpose: damage the player while hunger is below 5.
  - Target: health.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: -5 health every 5 seconds.

- sick
  - Display name: Food Poisoning.
  - Purpose: negative effect from spoiled food.
  - Target: health.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: -2 health every 5 seconds.
  - Display timer: ? because the duration is randomized.

- poisoned
  - Display name: Poisoned.
  - Purpose: stronger damage-over-time effect from toxins, enemies, or traps.
  - Suggested targets: health and stamina_regen.
  - Suggested mode: add.

- toxic_exposure
  - Display name: Toxic Exposure.
  - Purpose: environmental hazard from gas vents.
  - Target: health.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: -1 health per second while active.
  - Application: refreshed while the player is inside an active gas vent area.
  - Compatibility alias: gas.

- asphyxiation
  - Display name: Asphyxiation.
  - Purpose: stronger gas/smoke penalty after extended exposure.
  - Target: health.
  - Mode: effect-owned periodic edit.
  - Initial value: starts at -1 health per second. Gas behavior should ramp strength upward over time.
  - Application: starts after 10 continuous seconds in an active gas vent area and ramps slowly while exposure continues.
  - Compatibility alias: choking.

- bleeding
  - Display name: Bleeding.
  - Purpose: damage-over-time effect from wounds.
  - Suggested target: health.
  - Suggested mode: add.

- slowed
  - Display name: Encumbered.
  - Purpose: movement penalty from puddles, debris, injury, or other terrain.
  - Suggested target: movement_speed.
  - Suggested mode: add or set depending on source.

- stamina_boost
  - Display name: Endurance Boost.
  - Purpose: compatibility alias for endurance_boost until item data is migrated.

- refreshed
  - Display name: Refreshed.
  - Purpose: small recovery from drinking water.
  - Suggested targets: stamina, stamina_regen, or hunger_rate.
  - Suggested mode: add.

- bandaged
  - Display name: Bandaged.
  - Purpose: healing support and future bleeding removal.
  - Suggested targets: health.
  - Suggested mode: add.

- burning
  - Display name: Burning.
  - Purpose: fire damage from oil, explosions, or future fire sources.
  - Suggested target: health.
  - Suggested mode: add.

- blind
  - Display name: Blinded.
  - Purpose: temporary vision disruption, mostly for enemies later but supported for the player.
  - Suggested target: vision_radius.
  - Suggested mode: set or add.

- explosion
  - Display name: Blast Trauma.
  - Purpose: immediate blast effect from bombs.
  - Suggested behavior: applies health damage, noise, and possibly burning or choking.
  - Suggested mode: add.

- cure_poison
  - Display name: Antitoxin.
  - Purpose: removes or counteracts poisoned.
  - Suggested target: poisoned active effects.
  - Suggested mode: set or special-case removal.
