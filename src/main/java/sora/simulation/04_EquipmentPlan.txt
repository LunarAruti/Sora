Sora Equipment and Quick Use Plan
====================================

Scope
- This note captures the planned equipment, primary hand, secondary quick-slot, and apparel expansion systems.
- No runtime implementation is implied by this note until a later equipment pass starts.
- Equipment is different from normal items at the library/loot-table level, even though it behaves like items once it exists in inventory, containers, world drops, or equipment slots.

Core Controls
-------------
- F uses the primary held item.
- Number keys 1 through 6 use secondary quick-slot items.
- G swaps the primary slot with secondary slot 1 only when the player has the apparel/attachment that enables this behavior.
- G swap is active only during normal gameplay, not while inventory/container/document/oil targeting modes are open.
- At maximum capability, the player has one primary slot, six secondary slots, and 10x15 inventory space.

Primary Slot
------------
- The primary slot represents the item currently held in the player's hand.
- It can hold any item unless a future item-specific rule forbids it.
- Typical primary use examples:
  - Knife, spear, crowbar, rock, or similar physical items: swing/use in open air for future combat or object hits.
  - Portable lantern: turn on or turn off.
  - Food: eat.
  - Bandage: apply.
  - Lantern oil: start fill-lantern targeting mode.
  - Note/map fragment: read.
- The primary slot should be visually larger than secondary slots.

Secondary Quick Slots
---------------------
- Secondary slots represent items attached to the player for quick access.
- The player starts with 2 secondary slots.
- The maximum secondary slots is 6.
- Number keys activate the corresponding slot:
  - 1 uses secondary slot 1.
  - 2 uses secondary slot 2.
  - 3 through 6 work only when those slots are unlocked.
- By default, secondary slots can hold small consumables such as food, medical items, notes, map fragments, lantern oil, and flares.
- Apparel can expand the number of secondary slots and the kinds of items those slots accept.
- Secondary slots are for quick item use only. Tool-based contextual world actions require the tool in primary.

Inventory Size
--------------
- Default inventory size should become 10 wide by 5 high.
- Bags/backpacks expand inventory height in 5-row increments.
- Inventory must never exceed 10 wide by 15 high.
- Suggested starting inventory progression:
  - No bag: 10x5.
  - Satchel: 10x8.
  - Backpack: 10x12.
  - Heavy Backpack: 10x15 with movement noise.
- When inventory capacity shrinks and items no longer fit:
  - Try to move displaced items into remaining inventory space.
  - If no space exists, drop them near the player using the existing drop rules.
  - If dropping fails, show a notification and keep the apparel change from completing, unless the future design deliberately allows forced dropping.

Apparel/Capability Slots
------------------------
- The character equipment UI should show a small character/stickman region beneath active effects.
- Apparel/capability slots are drop targets placed around the character figure.
- Final planned apparel slots for now:
  - Back: satchel/backpack items that control inventory size.
  - Waist: belts that control secondary slot count and what those slots can hold.
  - Leg: holsters/leg pouches that enable primary/secondary swapping or add restricted quick access.
  - Chest: vests for defense.
  - Face: masks for gas protection.
  - Feet: shoes/boots for movement, terrain, and later noise behavior.
  - Hands: gloves for interaction capability.
  - Accessory: key ring, charm, watch, compass, and other small passive utility items.
- Shoulder equipment is intentionally excluded for now.
- Apparel should never push the player beyond max inventory or max secondary-slot caps.

Back Slot Variations
--------------------
- Satchel
  - Slot: back.
  - Inventory size: 10x8.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: smaller than backpack, probably lighter/quieter later.
- Backpack
  - Slot: back.
  - Inventory size: 10x12.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: standard medium storage.
- Heavy Backpack
  - Slot: back.
  - Inventory size: 10x15.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: maximum storage with added movement noise while moving.

Waist Slot Variations
---------------------
- Simple Belt
  - Slot: waist.
  - Adds +1 secondary slot.
  - Secondary allowance: all small consumables.
- Food Belt
  - Slot: waist.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: food only.
- Medical Belt
  - Slot: waist.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: medical items only.
- Utility Belt
  - Slot: waist.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: broad small-item access, including food, medical, notes, lantern oil, lockpicks, flares, rocks, and other non-large items.

Leg Slot Variations
-------------------
- Small Leg Pouch
  - Slot: leg.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: tiny items only.
- Food Pouch
  - Slot: leg.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: food only.
- Medical Pouch
  - Slot: leg.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: medical items only.
- Oil Pouch
  - Slot: leg.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Secondary allowance: lantern oil only.
- Leg Pouch
  - Slot: leg.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Enables G swap between primary and secondary slot 1.
  - Secondary allowance: small non-large items.
- Large Holster
  - Slot: leg.
  - Adds +2 secondary slots.
  - Enables G swap between primary and secondary slot 1.
  - Secondary allowance: any item that can be carried in a quick slot. This is one of the strongest leg-slot items.
  - Restriction: should allow larger carried items such as the portable lantern, but should not allow obviously huge items.

Chest Slot Variations
---------------------
- Light Vest
  - Slot: chest.
  - Capability: small defense increase later.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: no carry expansion.
- Reinforced Vest
  - Slot: chest.
  - Capability: stronger defense later.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: possible movement/noise penalty later.

Face Slot Variations
--------------------
- Cloth Mask
  - Slot: face.
  - Capability: weak gas exposure delay or reduction later.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: low protection.
- Protective Mask
  - Slot: face.
  - Capability: moderate gas protection.
- Filter Mask
  - Slot: face.
  - Capability: strongest gas protection.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: could require filters later if we add filter durability.

Feet Slot Variations
--------------------
- Worn Shoes
  - Slot: feet.
  - Capability: minor future terrain slow reduction for debris, rubble, bones, water, oil, and similar floor clutter.
- Work Boots
  - Slot: feet.
  - Capability: stronger future terrain slow reduction for debris, rubble, bones, water, oil, and similar floor clutter.
  - Tradeoff: adds movement noise while moving.
- Quiet Shoes
  - Slot: feet.
  - Capability: moderate future terrain slow reduction with lower movement noise.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: weaker terrain protection than boots.

Hands Slot Variations
---------------------
- Fingerless Gloves
  - Slot: hands.
  - Capability: +5 future search/drop-rate support.
- Cloth Gloves
  - Slot: hands.
  - Capability: +10 future search/drop-rate support and minor interaction safety later.
- Work Gloves
  - Slot: hands.
  - Capability: +15 future search/drop-rate support, better interaction safety, and future pry-time support.

Accessory Slot Variations
-------------------------
- Key Ring
  - Slot: accessory.
  - Capability: holds many keys outside normal inventory.
  - Restriction/tradeoff: only non-rusted keys.
  - Current behavior: stores non-rusted keys in a key-ring list while preserving key item id and key properties.
  - Current behavior: dragging a key onto a key ring removes it from inventory/container/equipment source and stores it in the key-ring list.
  - Current behavior: key-ring context info shows up to six stored keys at a time with simple up/down rows.
  - Current behavior: clicking a stored key removes it from the key ring and puts that key on the cursor for inventory placement.
  - Future behavior: when equipped in the accessory slot, use stored key ids for doors/chests/locks.
- Charm
  - Slot: accessory.
  - Capability: sanity-related passive behavior later.
- Watch
  - Slot: accessory.
  - Capability: future elapsed-time, effect timer, or fuel estimate UI helper.
- Compass
  - Slot: accessory.
  - Capability: future navigation/map helper.

Equipment Loot Model
--------------------
- Equipment should not have individual normal item spawn probability.
- Normal container generation should include one template entry that represents equipment as a whole.
- Suggested template id: equipment_template.
- The equipment template has a normal item loot weight.
- Starting equipment_template loot weight should match the crowbar tier, currently 2.0.
- When the normal loot table selects equipment_template, the generator rolls inside the equipment library to choose the actual equipment item.
- Each equipment item has equipment_weight for that second roll.
- This creates two layers:
  - Layer 1: normal loot decides whether this spawn is food, medical, tool, document, oil, equipment_template, etc.
  - Layer 2: equipment loot decides which actual equipment piece the equipment_template becomes.
- This prevents adding many equipment definitions from lowering the spawn rate of important normal items.
- Stronger or more flexible equipment should have lower equipment_weight.
- Simple/restricted equipment should have higher equipment_weight.
- Equipment generated inside a container is stored in that container's contents like any other item entry.
- Equipment should not be generated until the container is opened, matching the current item-seed container behavior.
- Equipment never stacks. Quantity is always 1.
- Tables are excluded from equipment drops.
- Bookshelves are excluded from equipment drops.
- Rubble is excluded from equipment drops.
- Bones can generate equipment.

Item Eligibility Ideas
----------------------
- Tiny items:
  - keys, lockpicks, notes, map fragments.
- Small consumables:
  - food, bandage, antitoxin, endurance draught, lantern oil, flare.
- Small tools:
  - rock, lockpick, possibly knife.
- Large hand items:
  - crowbar, spear, torch, shield, portable lantern.
- Default secondary slots should allow small consumables.
- Belts/pouches/special apparel can expand what item classes are allowed.
- Secondary slots can hold stacks.
- The primary slot can hold any item.
- Tool-based world secondary actions require the tool in primary, not merely in a secondary slot or inventory.
- A portable lantern emits light and applies Comfortable while on in primary.
- A portable lantern can emit from secondary only when the equipped apparel supports that behavior.

Inventory UI Plan
-----------------
- Inventory grid appears on the right as it does now, but its height depends on current inventory capacity.
- The left side keeps active effects at the top.
- Directly beneath active effects, draw the character equipment area.
- Apparel slots appear around the character figure.
- Directly beneath the character figure, draw the use slots:
  - one larger primary slot.
  - currently unlocked secondary slots in a row, up to 6.
- The primary slot should be the most visually prominent use slot because F uses it.
- Secondary slots should be smaller and ordered left to right as 1 through 6.
- If only two secondary slots are unlocked, only slots 1 and 2 should be visible for now.
- Locked secondary slots should either be hidden or shown dimmed; hiding is cleaner for now.
- Equipment slot boxes should be smaller than inventory cells so the little figure still fits behind/among them in the side panel.
- Empty equipment slot labels should appear under the boxes, not inside them.
- Equipped item hover should display the item name like inventory/container hover.
- Equipped item context menu should support at least unequip, drop, and use when valid.

Unequip and Capacity Rules
--------------------------
- Unequipping apparel can shrink inventory rows, secondary slot count, or slot eligibility.
- If removed apparel invalidates equipped/quick-slot items:
  - First try moving affected items into inventory.
  - If inventory has no space, try dropping them near the player.
  - If neither succeeds, block the unequip and notify the player.
- Dropped items must use the same unified persistent item-state behavior as current inventory drops.

Open Questions
--------------
- When pressing 1 through 6 on a stack of food, should one item be consumed and the stack stay equipped?
- Should two-handed items exist later, blocking shield/primary combinations?
- Movement noise should be a running value from 0 to 1000, not a multiplier.
- Equipment can contribute flat movement-noise values while the player is moving.
- Face masks can contribute small constant breathing noise while worn.

Implementation Plan
===================

Pass 1: Equipment Data Model
----------------------------
- Add a dedicated equipment definition model/library separate from DungeonItemLibrary.
- Equipment definitions should include:
  - id
  - public name
  - apparel/body slot
  - inventory size
  - visual
  - equipment_weight
  - properties/capabilities
- Equipment can reuse the same runtime inventory entry shape as items:
  - item_id
  - x
  - y
  - quantity
  - properties
- Runtime code needs lookup helpers that can resolve both normal items and equipment by id.
- Add equipment slot concepts:
  - primary
  - secondary 1 through secondary 6
  - back
  - waist
  - leg
  - chest
  - face
  - feet
  - hands
  - accessory
- Add an equipment-state object owned by MazeSimulationGame.
- Equipped items are removed from the inventory grid and stored only in the equipment state.
- Secondary equipped items may keep stack quantity.
- Add helper rules for:
  - current inventory capacity from back item.
  - current unlocked secondary-slot count from waist/leg/chest/etc.
  - whether an item fits a secondary slot.
  - whether an apparel item fits a body slot.
  - whether G swap is enabled.
- Keep the current max caps strict:
  - inventory max 10x15.
  - secondary max 6.
  - one primary slot.

Pass 2: Equipment Definitions
-----------------------------
- Add apparel/equipment definitions to the equipment library, not the normal item library.
- Suggested definitions:
  - satchel
  - backpack
  - heavy_backpack
  - simple_belt
  - food_belt
  - medical_belt
  - utility_belt
  - small_leg_pouch
  - food_pouch
  - medical_pouch
  - oil_pouch
  - leg_pouch
  - holster / Large Holster
  - light_vest
  - reinforced_vest
  - cloth_mask
  - protective_mask
  - filter_mask
  - worn_shoes
  - work_boots
  - quiet_shoes
  - fingerless_gloves
  - cloth_gloves
  - work_gloves
  - key_ring
  - charm
  - watch
  - compass
- Add properties for apparel:
  - apparel_slot
  - inventory_rows
  - secondary_slots
  - secondary_allowance

  - gas_protection
  - terrain_slow_reduction
  - movement_noise
  - breathing_noise
  - defense
  - search_bonus
  - pry_time_bonus
  - key_capacity
- Add visuals and equipment weights.
- Keep rare/strong capability items lower equipment_weight than simple pouches/belts.

Pass 3: Container Loot Integration
----------------------------------
- Add one normal item loot-table entry for equipment_template, currently weighted at 18.0.
- When equipment_template is selected during container generation:
  - choose an actual equipment definition using equipment_weight.
  - create a container entry using that selected equipment id.
  - place it using the same grid placement rules as normal items.
- Equipment entries in containers should support:
  - hover name.
  - drag/drop.
  - move to inventory.
  - drop.
  - context menu.
  - Use/Equip action.
- Container persistence should store the final selected equipment id, not equipment_template.
- Opened containers should preserve equipment contents exactly like normal item contents.
- Equipment should not dilute normal loot beyond the single equipment_template entry.
- Exclude equipment_template from table and rubble loot pools.
- Exclude equipment_template from bookshelf loot pools.
- Allow equipment_template in bones and normal storage containers.
- Force generated equipment quantity to 1.

Pass 4: Inventory Capacity Shrink
---------------------------------
- Change default inventory size to 10x5.
- Make inventory rendering and placement use current capacity instead of hard-coded max capacity.
- When equipping/removing back items changes capacity:
  - If capacity grows, just resize available inventory space.
  - If capacity shrinks, detect inventory items outside the new bounds.
  - Try moving displaced items into valid free cells.
  - If they cannot fit, try dropping them near the player.
  - If neither works, reject the equipment change and notify the player.
- Existing container inventories remain unaffected.

Pass 5: Equipment UI
--------------------
- In the inventory overlay, keep active effects at the upper-left.
- Beneath effects, draw the character equipment area.
- Draw body slots:
  - back
  - waist
  - leg
  - chest
  - face
  - feet
  - hands
  - accessory
- Draw use slots beneath the body area:
  - one large primary slot.
  - visible secondary slots based on current unlocked count.
- Support hover name display for equipped items.
- Support dragging items from inventory/container into equipment slots.
- Support dragging equipped items back into inventory/container.
- Support context menu actions on equipped items:
  - Use
  - Unequip
  - Drop

Pass 6: Equip and Unequip Rules
-------------------------------
- Equip from context menu should:
  - put equipment/apparel into its matching body slot only if that slot is empty.
  - put normal items into the next available use slot, checking primary first and then secondary slots in order.
  - reject with a notification when no legal empty slot exists.
  - never swap items.
- Equipment context menus should show only Equip before equipped, not both Use and Equip.
- Items that can be used and equipped, such as lantern oil, should show both Use and Equip.
- Items that can only be equipped, such as crowbar, should show only Equip.
- Dragging an item onto an occupied equipment/use slot can swap when both items are legal in their new slots.
- Dragging an equipped item back into inventory/container should move it out of equipment.
- Equipped items should support context menu actions:
  - Use when the item has a wired use action.
  - Unequip.
  - Drop.
- Future primary-use behavior may allow held equipment to equip into its apparel slot, but context-menu Equip should stay no-swap.
- Dragging should determine exact target slot.
- If a target equipment slot is occupied:
  - swap when legal.
  - otherwise reject with notification.
- Unequipping should try inventory first, then world drop, then reject if no legal destination exists.
- Dropping equipped items should use the same drop rules and persistence as inventory drops.

Pass 7: Primary and Secondary Use Hotkeys
-----------------------------------------
- F uses the primary item.
- 1 through 6 use the matching unlocked secondary slot.
- F and 1 through 6 only use equipped items during normal gameplay.
- F and 1 through 6 should not use equipped items while inventory, container, document, or targeting modes are open.
- Using an equipped stack consumes one quantity and leaves the remaining stack equipped.
- Primary use should call the same item-use behavior as inventory context Use where possible:
  - food eats.
  - bandage applies.
  - antitoxin drinks.
  - endurance draught drinks.
  - lantern oil enters fill-lantern mode.
  - note/map fragment reads.
  - lantern toggles on/off.
- If lantern oil is used while a portable lantern is equipped in primary, it should fill that primary lantern immediately instead of entering selection mode.
- If the primary lantern is already full, oil should not be consumed.
- If lantern oil is used from an equipped slot during normal gameplay and no primary lantern is equipped, show a notification instead of entering selection mode.
- Weapons/tools without concrete behavior should show a notification such as Swung Crowbar.

Pass 8: Primary Tool World Interactions
---------------------------------------
- Contextual world secondary actions should check the primary item only.
- Pry behavior is deferred until locked-object interactions exist.
- Pry should only appear for locked objects and should likely occupy that object's primary interaction slot.
- Crowbar should be the main pry tool once implemented.
- Knife can also pry, but should have a significantly lower success chance.
- Until lock/pry mechanics exist, crowbar and knife should not alter chest, barrel, crate, or rubble prompts.

Pass 9: Equipped Light Behavior
-------------------------------
- Portable lantern in primary emits light centered on the player when on and fueled.
- Portable lantern in primary applies Comfortable while on and fueled.
- Portable lantern in secondary emits light only from secondary slot 1 when equipped apparel allows secondary light.
- Lantern fuel drains while equipped and on, including while inventory or container menus are open.
- Inventory-stored lanterns still do nothing.
- Dropped lanterns remain off by default.

Pass 10: Apparel Capability Hooks - Deferred
--------------------------------------------
- Hold off on most gameplay effects caused by worn apparel until after the current equipment/inventory passes are solidified.
- Active now: movement_noise affects current noise while moving; breathing_noise affects current noise constantly while the mask is worn.
- Deferred: damage/defense changes, strength or interaction changes, gas/asphyxiation changes, movement/terrain slow changes, and other stat modifiers caused by wearing equipment.
- Apparel definitions can keep capability metadata for display/planning, but equipping apparel should not actively alter those systems yet.
- Face masks can later alter, decrease, or delay gas/asphyxiation effects. Ask for exact gas-mask behavior before implementing these rules.
- Feet items can later modify terrain slow behavior further; basic puddle/bone/rubble slow is wired.
- Feet and other movement-noise apparel now modify current noise while moving.
- Character noise is now an active running number on the character state, not a timed effect; movement_noise and breathing_noise apparel properties are active.
- Vest items can later provide defense once damage reduction is wired.
- Gloves can later affect interaction/pry/search mechanics.
- Accessory key ring can later store keys once key/lock behavior is wired.
- Watch/compass/charm remain capability placeholders until their systems exist.

Pass 11: Persistence and Memory Rules
-------------------------------------
- Equipped items are player-state objects, not loaded chunk objects.
- Equipment in unopened containers does not exist until generated on first open.
- Equipment in opened containers exists only in that container's persistent contents.
- Equipment dropped into the world uses the unified persistent item-state map as a placed world item.
- Equipment removed from inventory/container during equip must be removed from that source immediately.
- If a chunk unloads, world-dropped equipment should leave active world lists just like dropped normal items.
- Persistent item state is the single map-delta list for changed/deleted seed items and placed/dropped world items.
- Placed/dropped entries store the item id, cell coordinate, direction, quantity, and properties needed to recreate them when the chunk reloads.
- Placed/dropped entries use a separate persistent key namespace from seed-spawned entries so a placed item cannot overwrite a seed deletion marker at the same cell.
- Seed-spawned changed/deleted objects reload from their seed identity, while placed/dropped items reload from their stored cell coordinate/properties.
- Untouched seed-default map items should not create persistent item-state entries.
- Seeded map lights that spawn off should not enter persistent item state unless toggled or otherwise changed.
- Seeded map lights that spawn on may enter persistent item state once their fuel starts draining, because fuel is then changed from the seed default.
- A lightweight ownership audit should run occasionally and log suspicious duplicates, but it should not repair or delete anything automatically.

Pass 12: Review and Cleanup
---------------------------
- Verify equipment state clears on close.
- Verify equipped items do not also exist in inventory/container lists.
- Verify max inventory and max secondary slots cannot be exceeded.
- Verify shrinking capacity cannot lose items silently.
- Verify dropped equipped items use unified persistent item state.
- Verify world interactions are disabled while inventory/container/document/oil selection modes are open.
