PIXEL GENERATOR PALETTE

Purpose
- Canonical token reference for the fixed pixel-generator palette.
- Tokens are case-insensitive after normalization.
- Leading and trailing whitespace is ignored before lookup.
- `.` and the transparent aliases below resolve to full transparency.
- All color tokens below are fully opaque unless explicitly placed in the Transparent section.
